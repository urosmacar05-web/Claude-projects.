The Blue Cave Tour

Three hours, stop by stop

From the walls of Kotor to the mouth of the bay and back.

  2. 00

    Kotor · Park Slobode

    Board the boat at Park Slobode in Kotor.

  3. 01

    Perast & Our Lady of the Rock

    Baroque palaces and bell towers, and an island church built on a reef that sailors raised stone by stone.

  4. 02

    Submarine Tunnels

    Hidden shelters carved into the cliffs, where the Yugoslav navy once kept its submarines.

  5. 03

    Mamula Island

    A round 19th-century fortress guarding the entrance to the Bay of Kotor.

  6. 04

    Swim in the Blue Cave

    Sunlight enters beneath the waterline and lights the cave a glowing blue. Jump in — the snorkelling gear is on board.

Ask on WhatsApp
Where does the tour start?

All tours depart from Park Slobode in Kotor. Please arrive a few minutes before your departure time.

What is included?

Snorkelling equipment and bottled water are included on every tour. On the private boat we also provide a bottle of champagne.

Can I bring food and drinks?

Yes — you are welcome to bring your own food and drinks on board.

What should I bring?

Swimwear, a towel and sunscreen for the Blue Cave swim — plus your own food and drinks if you like.

How does the private boat work?

A private boat costs €130 per hour for your whole group. Tell us how long you'd like and which places you want to see, and we'll arrange it with you.

How do I book?
